Grandma's Apple Pie

  • 2 Pie Crusts
  • 6-8 Granny Smith Apples, peeled and sliced thin
  • 1 tbsp Lemon Juice
  • ¾ cup Light Brown Sugar, packed
  • ¼ tsp Salt
  • 1 tbsp Ground Cinnamon
  • 1 tsp Ground Nutmeg
  • ¼ cup All-purpose Flour
  • ¼ cup Unsalted Butter, melted and cooled
  • Heavy Cream, brushed on top before baking
